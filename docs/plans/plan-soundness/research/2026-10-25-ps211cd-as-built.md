# PS2.11c and PS2.11d as built (2026-10-25)

Record for cards PS2.11c (`W_FOOTPRINT_TRANSPOSED`) and PS2.11d (`W_LEAF_ACROSS_DOORWAY`),
PR #253. Both are validity-layer lint rules appended last to `LINT_RULES` in this order, and both
are classified `invariant` in the equivariance oracle (`test/d4-oracle.ts`). Their tests are in
`test/lint-clearances-transposed-leaf.test.ts`.

## PS2.11c · `W_FOOTPRINT_TRANSPOSED`

`src/lint/rules/footprint.ts`.

- **Which families.** Only those that come in one shape. The catalogue marks them with the
  `fixedAspect` flag (`src/fixtures-catalog.ts`), and each entry gives its reason in a line:
  bed, bunk bed, crib/cot, bath (and `tub`, `bathtub`), WC (and `toilet`), bidet,
  `sofa_l`/`corner_sofa`, loveseat/`sofa_2`, bar counter, TV, mirror and treadmill. (`chaise`
  carried it until review round 2: a chaise longue has its back at a short end as often as along
  a long side, and `drawChaise` draws 800 × 1600 correctly.)
  A family sold in both shapes is never judged: wardrobe (a single door is deeper than wide),
  filing cabinet (lateral or vertical), locker, shed, sink, desk and the rest. A guard test holds
  every flagged footprint to at least 5:4 and not symmetric.
  - *Review round 1 (B1):* the first version judged every directional family minus `bookshelf`
    and `radiator`. It fired on standard products that draw correctly: `filing_cabinet 900x450`,
    `locker 300x450`, `shed 1800x2400` (whose fix would have turned the building),
    `wardrobe 450x600` and `kitchen_sink 450x600`.
  - *Bar counter, a choice beyond the reviewer's candidate list:* a counter with stools along its
    front is always wider than deep, since the stools need its length. It keeps C4 caught.
- **The test.** It reads the frame the symbol is drawn in: `size`, with its sides swapped under
  `rotate 90|270`, and along × depth for `against`. It fires when that frame has its long side
  where the catalogue footprint has its short one, by at least 5:4 (`4 · long ≥ 5 · short`,
  exact).
  - This compares aspect classes. It is not the card's "the swap reproduces the catalogue aspect
    within 5 %", which would have caught neither in-reach audit instance: RS4 is 12 % off the
    catalogue aspect and RK4 39 %.
  - A `place` turn or mirror carries the frame unchanged.
- **Message.** The closing clause comes from the catalogue's own orientation. A wider-than-deep
  piece "has its long side drawn as its depth" (`sofa_l`); a deeper-than-wide one "has its long
  side drawn along its back" (bed, WC). The pieces are named by label or kind, rooms by label.
- **Hint.** It quotes the piece's id only when the author wrote one; otherwise it says "the
  `<kind>` in `<room>`".
- **Fix.** Offered only when `size` is one literal token and the swapped size, as drawn on the
  grid, is exactly the catalogue footprint. It is `maybe-incorrect`, because the swap changes the
  floor the piece covers.
  - The literal's span is carried as `_sizeSwap`, through the AST field `sizeSpan`. That field
    appears in `archlang ast --json`, like `rotateSpan`, and never in the drawing, `describe()` or
    Plan JSON.
- **Out of reach.**
  - A plain `sofa` has no catalogued footprint (PS3.11), so `clearances/sofa-transposed` stays
    `todo`.
  - A 1.8 m-deep wardrobe is out of reach too (card PS2.53: a per-family depth ceiling).

## PS2.11d · `W_LEAF_ACROSS_DOORWAY`

In `src/lint/rules/doors.ts`.

- **The defect.** A hinged door's open leaf, parked where it is drawn (`doorSwing`, from the
  hinge to the open tip), stands in another door's approach.
  - The approach is the other door's span, jamb to jamb, `doorwayLandingMm` deep (300; 450 under
    `accessibility-advisory`). It is measured from that door's wall face on the leaf's side,
    closed at the face and open at the far edge.
  - It fires when the leaf leaves less of the doorway clear than `min(that doorway's width,
    minDoorWidthMm)`.
  - Both doors must open onto the room the leaf parks in: the access graph's `between`, and the
    mid-point of the leaf's run in the zone on that room's floor.
- **Datum.** `W_DOORWAY_BLOCKED` measures the same depth from the wall's centreline. Read from
  there, the audits' A1 and NH5 sit exactly on the zone's edge. Card PS2.54 unifies the two.
- **Not judged.**
  - A pair whose swings collide: that is `W_SWING_OBSTRUCTED`'s report. The gap this leaves is
    card PS2.51.
  - A leaf-less opening as the approached doorway.
  - A door on an angled or curved wall, in either role: its leaf or its approach. Tests cover
    both directions.
- **Message.** It names both doors by the rooms they join; ids appear only in hints, and only
    when the author wrote them. No fix.
